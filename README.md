# Free CV Builder

A single-page CV builder. No sign-up, no server, no cost. Everything runs in your browser.

## Run on your device
Keep the three files together in one folder and double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser. That's it.

## Put it on GitHub (free website)
1. Create a new repository on github.com.
2. Upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `script.js` and `README.md`.
3. Go to Settings > Pages, choose the `main` branch and `/ (root)`, then Save.
4. Your site will be live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/REPO-NAME/`.

## Make a PDF
Click "Save as PDF / Print" and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination.

Your CV is saved in your browser only (localStorage), never uploaded anywhere.

## Typing in Experience and Education
- Start a line with `-` or `•` to make a bullet.
- Press Enter to move to a new line. A line right under a bullet stays inside that bullet.
- Leave a blank line to add a space.
